# *Obsidian Craft* v0.1 (*Fabric*)

## This is a mod about Obsidian.

In this first version, I added **Obsidian ingot**, **block**, **tool**, **armor** and **furnace**.
- The **Obsidian Ingot** can be smelted from Obsidian or Crying Obsidian. Four ingots can craft a block.
- The **Obsidian Tools** have the durability about twice as the iron tools. Specially, the **Obsidian Pickaxe** can mine Obsidian (not only) as approximately fast as Diamond Pickaxe with efficiency III. What's more, it can **repair** itself when mining these blocks.
- The **Obsidian Armors** can provide you fire protection, which contains reducing the damage and the fire remaining time.
- The **Obsidian Furnace** can give you faster smelting speed as **Blast Furnace**, but it can smelt **all** the furnace recipes more than blasting recipes. It will have more applications in the future version.
